The water cycle describes how water moves through the environment. Water evaporates from oceans, lakes and rivers, rises into the atmosphere as vapor, cools and condenses into clouds, and finally returns to the surface as rain or snow. Part of this water flows into rivers and back to the sea, while another part soaks into the ground and feeds underground aquifers. Plants also release water vapor through their leaves, a process called transpiration.
Il ciclo dell'acqua descrive il modo in cui l'acqua si muove nell'ambiente. L'acqua evapora da oceani, laghi e fiumi, sale nell'atmosfera come vapore, si raffredda e si condensa in nuvole, e infine torna sulla superficie come pioggia o neve.
